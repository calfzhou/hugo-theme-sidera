Use a branch bundle when a document may gain children. Its body remains ordinary Markdown.

[Continue to the example](example/_index.md).


## Shared taxonomy fields

Use ordinary top-level `tags` and `categories` arrays on posts, notes and docs.
A regular page or leaf bundle can move between collections without converting
those fields. Hierarchy is a site/owner presentation policy, not another schema.
The repository's TAXONOMIES.md documents global/scoped views and the bounded local
route-discovery contract; arbitrary mounted taxonomy discovery is not implied by
this sample's opt-in documentation mount.
